About me

Academic visit I am currently seeking a 3–6 month academic visiting opportunity, with funding fully covered by my PhD programme in Italy.

Hi, I am Zanxi. I am a second-year PhD candidate at the University of Verona, supervised by Prof. Marco Cristani and Dr. Yiming Wang.

My research focuses on multimodal representation learning, particularly the geometry, alignment, and biases of vision-language representations. I am particularly interested in extending this work toward embodied intelligence, with a focus on robust perception, multimodal reasoning, and transferable representations for interactive agents.

Previously, I earned my Master's degree from the National University of Defense Technology and my Bachelor's degree, ranked 1st of 70, from North China University of Technology.
